# Family school-holiday planning worksheet

Start with the school’s confirmed dates, then choose activities. Government term dates alone do not establish a private school’s break or an organiser’s session availability.

## Copy and complete

| Planning item | Family note |
|---|---|
| School, campus, year level | To be confirmed by the family |
| Last actual attendance date | Check the school calendar |
| First actual return date | Check orientation and student-free days |
| Public holidays and pupil-free days | Check school and state sources |
| Days needing care and transport | Record dates, hours and pickup arrangements |
| Total budget | Include fees, materials, travel, care and food |
| One booked activity | Confirm age range, dates, supervision and cancellation terms |
| One flexible outing | Check opening hours and accessibility |
| One home backup | Choose a small project with a clear finished result |
| Child’s choice and rest time | Leave space for energy, interests and change |

## Choosing a learning activity

Ask what the child will finish, whether a complete example is viewable, how adults provide feedback, what devices/accounts are required and how pickup or online supervision works. Confirm the session rather than assuming that every school-holiday date is available.
