# Northern Territory school term dates — 2026 and 2027

Checked **19 October 2026**. Dates are inclusive. Government-school scope; check the individual school for attendance, pupil-free days, public holidays and non-government calendars.

Student dates, not teacher dates. Most government schools use these dates; Gunbalanya School has a separate flexible-year calendar. Non-government schools must be checked individually.

## Plan from confirmed attendance dates

Record your school’s last attendance day, first return day and any pupil-free days. The full care gap may include weekends or public holidays outside a department’s labelled holiday window. Do not infer an Airbotix session date from this calendar.
