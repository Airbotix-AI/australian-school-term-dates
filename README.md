# Australian School Term Dates 2026–2027

A Markdown reference for families planning school terms, holidays and learning activities across all **eight Australian states and territories**, maintained by [Airbotix](https://airbotix.ai/).

**Checked 19 October 2026 (Australia/Brisbane).** Government-school dates only. Where government sources distinguish student starts, we use those; WA, SA and TAS retain published term boundaries with exceptions below. These are not every school’s attendance calendar. Catholic/independent schools, public holidays, pupil-free days, orientation and senior exams can change the dates a child actually attends.

Dates use `YYYY-MM-DD`; endpoints are inclusive. Each row links its government source. No JSON, CSV, executable code, account or download is required.

## Before booking

- NSW has two Term 1 start dates; ACT separates new and continuing students.
- Victoria’s Term 1 student start differs from the formal term boundary.
- WA’s 2027 Term 2 boundary has a public-holiday caveat; TAS’s 2027 Term 3 boundary is a K–10 student-free day.
- SA’s source has a 2027 return/summer-holiday overlap. Confirm with the school.
- Check the school calendar before booking travel, care or activities. Dates here do not prove a camp runs or places are available.
