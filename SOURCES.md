## Editorial method

Transcribe dates as published, distinguishing student attendance from staff/term boundaries where stated. Preserve source caveats and conflicting details rather than silently resolving them. NSW divisions are separate variants; they do not add a ninth jurisdiction. No holiday intervals or 2028 dates are guessed. Verify school-specific attendance and current public holidays before decisions.

These are dated observations, not a live feed or an automated update promise. A checked date does not guarantee a source will remain unchanged. Corrections require a primary source, affected year, school system and date basis. No CI, scheduled job or paid API is included.
